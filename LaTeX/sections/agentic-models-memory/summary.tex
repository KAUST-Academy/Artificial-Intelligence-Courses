\begin{frame}{Research Questions to Take with You}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.35em
        \item RL for stronger reasoning raises tool hallucination in step with task gains. \textbf{What reward teaches an agent to say ``I cannot do this''} without lowering its utility?
        \item Memory write policies are learned from a final-answer reward on question answering. \textbf{Does that transfer to long-running work?}
        \item Much of the multi-agent gain is parallel compute. \textbf{What is the right matched-budget baseline}, and what is left after it?
        \item LLM world models are good one-step critics and poor simulators. \textbf{Can a world model be trained to be pessimistic}, not sycophantic?
        \item The 80\% time horizon is a small fraction of the 50\% one. \textbf{What closes that gap}: training, verification, or operations?
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
