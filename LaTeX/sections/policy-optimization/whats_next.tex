\begin{frame}{What's Next}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Key limitation:} on-policy methods discard all experience after each update --- data-hungry for complex environments
    \pause
    \item \textbf{DDPG \& TD3:} off-policy deterministic policies for continuous control --- replay buffer, action-value critic $Q_\phi(s,a)$, gradient through $Q_\phi(s,\mu_\theta(s))$
    \item \textbf{SAC:} max-entropy Actor-Critic --- the same $c_2\mathcal{H}(\pi_\theta)$ bonus you saw in PPO, now \textbf{central} to the objective
    \item \textbf{Exploration:} that entropy bonus \emph{is} exploration via a stochastic policy; later we make exploration the explicit objective
    \item \textbf{RLHF:} PPO is the algorithm underneath the original InstructGPT/ChatGPT pipeline; GRPO is its critic-free successor
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
