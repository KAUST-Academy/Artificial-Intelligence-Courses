    \takeaway{The common thread: \textbf{inference compute is only as useful as the signal that selects or corrects its output.} Since 2025 that signal has moved into training, and what is left for the user is an effort dial.}

\begin{frame}{Research Questions to Take with You}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.5em
        \item Outside verifiable domains, selection is the bottleneck. \textbf{What is a good verifier for open-ended work}, where reward models correlate with quality at 0.12?
        \item Does RL \textbf{create} new search ability, or mostly make sampling from the base model more efficient? What experiment would separate the two?
        \item When traces get shorter, is that \textbf{efficiency or hidden computation}? How should compute then be reported?
        \item Can a chain of thought stay \textbf{monitorable} under ever stronger outcome optimisation, without paying for it in capability?
        \item Formal provers now write their own statements. \textbf{Who checks that the statement is the right one?}
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
