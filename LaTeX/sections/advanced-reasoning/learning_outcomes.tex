\section{Learning Outcomes}

\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    By the end of this session, you should be able to:
    \vspace{0.2em}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.2em
        \item \textbf{Explain} why test-time compute scales log-linearly, and why the verifier bounds it.
        \item \textbf{Compare} Least-to-Most and Graph-of-Thought with chains and trees, and rank selectors beyond the vote.
        \item \textbf{Analyse} best-of-N, beam-style search and MCTS, and when search beats sampling.
        \item \textbf{Contrast} process and outcome reward models, at inference and inside RL.
        \item \textbf{Describe} how code execution, symbolic engines and Lean make a check exact.
        \item \textbf{Diagnose} when Self-Refine, Reflexion and related loops help, and when they fail.
        \item \textbf{Evaluate} planning claims and the 2026 reasoning models from public evidence.
    \end{itemize}
    \vspace{0.1em}
    {\footnotesize \textbf{Prerequisite knowledge:} chain-of-thought prompting, self-consistency, Tree-of-Thought, PAL, pass@$k$; outcome and process reward models, reward over-optimisation, RL with verifiable rewards, GRPO; DeepSeek-R1 and R1-Zero's ``aha moment''; overthinking and the Illusion of Thinking; unfaithful and monitorable chain-of-thought traces; compute-optimal and over-trained scaling.\par}
\end{frame}
