\begin{frame}{Research Questions to Take with You}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.45em
        \item Speculative decoding and diffusion decoding are reported at low batch. \textbf{What survives at production batch sizes?}
        \item Can a model be \textbf{updated at Wikipedia's rate}, half a million facts in five months, with consequences propagated and sources attached?
        \item \textbf{What training objective connects uncertainty to abstention} without collapsing coverage?
        \item \textbf{What makes a judge safe to optimise against?}
        \item For Arabic and its dialects: \textbf{where in the network is the knowledge lost}, and does a targeted intervention beat more data?
    \end{itemize}
    \vspace{0.3em}
    \begin{block}{Three questions, one lesson}
        Where computation lives: on the device, in a drafter, in a parallel denoiser. Where memory lives: in weights, in tables, in context. How it is checked: by uncertainty, from inside, by judges, and never by a number the model was trained to raise.
    \end{block}
\end{frame}
