\begin{frame}{Frontier Models: What to Remember}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.55em
        \item \textbf{Closed flagships} share a shape: 1M context, graded always-on reasoning, staged access. No architecture is disclosed.
        \item \textbf{Open flagships} are trillion-parameter sparse MoEs with open weights, but rarely open data.
        \item \textbf{No leaderboard defines ``best''.} Three trackers give three orders, and each has a known weakness.
        \item Open models are \textbf{months behind in time} and about \textbf{10 index points behind in level}.
        \item \textbf{Arabic-capable open models} are orders of magnitude smaller, with short context.
    \end{itemize}
    \vspace{0.4em}
    \takeaway{\textbf{Next.} The models are stronger every month, and the instruments that say so keep failing. That tension is where the open problems are.}
\end{frame}
