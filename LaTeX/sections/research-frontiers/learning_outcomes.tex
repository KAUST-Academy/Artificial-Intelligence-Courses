\section{Learning Outcomes}

\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    By the end of this session, you should be able to:
    \vspace{0.3em}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.15em
        \item \textbf{Explain} what makes a small model strong in 2026, and what limits it on a device.
        \item \textbf{Compare} drafter-free speculative decoding, token pruning and weight compression by their \emph{realised} speedups.
        \item \textbf{Describe} how diffusion language models are built and accelerated, and where they trail autoregression.
        \item \textbf{Analyse} parametric memory: its capacity, and its freshness and attribution limits.
        \item \textbf{Derive} why binary grading rewards guessing, and choose an uncertainty estimator.
        \item \textbf{Evaluate} what probes, sparse autoencoders and steering vectors can and cannot show.
        \item \textbf{Assess} judges, reward models and leaderboards as instruments that degrade when optimised.
        \item \textbf{Formulate} a research question from the October 2026 frontier.
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
