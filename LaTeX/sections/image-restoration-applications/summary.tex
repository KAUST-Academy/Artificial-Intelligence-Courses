\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Summary}
  \begin{itemize}\scriptsize\itemsep0.35em
    \item \textbf{Restoration inverts $y = \mathcal{A}(x) + n$, and the inversion is ill-posed.} An MSE loss returns the blurry average of all plausible images; perception and distortion cannot both be optimal, so report a fidelity metric (PSNR, SSIM) next to a perceptual one. Real photos need realistic synthetic degradations, and Real-ESRGAN's second-order pipeline is still the default training recipe.
    \item \textbf{Real-world super-resolution now runs on generative priors.} Regression backbones gained 0.62\,dB on Urban100 from SwinIR to MambaIRv2, with about $3\times$ the parameters; StableSR, DiffBIR and SUPIR steer a frozen diffusion prior with the low-quality input; OSEDiff does it in one step (0.11\,s against 11.5\,s for StableSR); transformer and flow priors and one-step video models (SeedVR2, FlashVSR) are the 2025--2026 frontier. Generated detail can be wrong, so keep the input next to the output.
    \item \textbf{Real noise and blur break synthetic training, and clean targets are optional.} Raw noise is Poisson-Gaussian and the ISP reshapes it; Restormer and MambaIR backbones still lead the 2026 PSNR challenge; Noise2Noise trains on noisy pairs; frequency-domain and state-space models reach 34.2--34.5\,dB on GoPro deblurring; instruction-driven and agentic restorers pick the operation; posterior sampling (DPS) reuses one diffusion prior for any known degradation.
    \item \textbf{Filling a hole and removing an object are different tasks.} LaMa's global receptive field handles large masks in one pass; diffusion models are conditioned through extra input channels, RePaint-style resampling or plug-in branches (BrushNet); removing shadows and reflections with the object needs counterfactual data (ObjectDrop, ROSE for video), and RORem scales up curated removal pairs.
    \item \textbf{Industrial inspection is one-class detection with pixel-level localisation.} Patch-memory $k$-NN (PatchCore) and student-teacher discrepancy (EfficientAD, 2.2\,ms) remain strong; Dinomaly trains one DINOv2-based model per dataset; CLIP- and DINOv2-based detectors work zero- or few-shot; MLLMs still trail human inspectors on MMAD. Score defects with PRO, set thresholds on normal data, and expect large drops on MVTec AD 2.
    \item \textbf{In medical imaging, configuration and validation matter as much as architecture.} nnU-Net derives its pipeline from the dataset, and in a controlled re-benchmark properly configured CNN U-Nets beat newer transformer and Mamba designs; TotalSegmentator runs locally; promptable 3D models cut annotation time per lesion from 525.9\,s to 74.3\,s in one study; pathology uses DINOv2-style tile encoders plus attention MIL; MedGemma is an open medical VLM with stated limits. Generative reconstruction can invent or erase lesions.
    \item \textbf{Earth-observation models must handle bands, time and place.} SatMAE, Prithvi-EO-2.0, DOFA and TerraMind encode spectra, dates and locations; AlphaEarth Foundations releases a 64-dimensional embedding for every 10\,m pixel, so a map needs only labels and a small classifier; PANGAEA finds that the models it tests (DOFA, CROMA, Prithvi 1.0 and others) do not consistently beat a supervised U-Net, while TerraMind reports beating it.
    \item \textbf{Traffic measurement is detection plus tracking plus geometry; driving plans need simulation-based scoring.} SORT and ByteTrack combine Kalman prediction, Hungarian matching and low-score boxes; a homography turns tracks into speeds; LSS and BEVFormer build bird's-eye-view features; UniAD trains the stack end to end, but an ego-status MLP matches it on open-loop nuScenes, so NAVSIM (non-reactive) and Bench2Drive (closed loop) score plans in simulation; the 2025 policies shown here are a truncated diffusion planner (DiffusionDrive) and VLA models (EMMA, Alpamayo-R1).
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
