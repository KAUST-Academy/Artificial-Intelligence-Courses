\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
  By the end of this lecture you should be able to:
  \begin{itemize}\small\itemsep0.2em
    \item \textbf{Write} the pinhole projection and back-projection, and explain calibration and homographies.
    \item \textbf{Derive} the epipolar constraint and recover relative pose and 3D points despite outliers.
    \item \textbf{Compute} depth from disparity and its error, and explain cost volumes and learned stereo.
    \item \textbf{Compare} monocular depth estimators by their training signal and evaluation protocol.
    \item \textbf{Estimate} absolute camera pose and explain structure from motion with bundle adjustment.
    \item \textbf{Describe} a visual SLAM system and contrast feature-based, direct, and learned SLAM.
    \item \textbf{Explain} how feed-forward 3D models replace parts of the classical pipeline, and their limits.
  \end{itemize}
  \vspace{0.2em}
  \begin{itemize}\footnotesize
    \item \textbf{Prerequisite knowledge:} CNN encoder--decoders, GRUs, 3D convolutions; ViT, cross-attention, DINOv2, MAE; Stable Diffusion; optical flow, image retrieval; RGB-D, LiDAR; SVD; Depth Anything, SuperGlue.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
