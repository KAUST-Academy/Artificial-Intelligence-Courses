\begin{frame}{Research Questions to Take with You}
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.65em
        \item Random rewards improve one model family and not others. \textbf{What exactly does RL with verifiable rewards teach}, beyond sharpening what pre-training left?
        \item Monitors, judges and reviewers all erode under optimisation. Is there an oversight signal whose reliability \textbf{grows} with the capability of the model it checks?
        \item Is there a \textbf{scaling law for synthetic data}: how much, from which teacher, at what share, before diversity is lost?
        \item Can a self-improvement loop be shown to exceed its source data, or is every gain \textbf{amortised search}?
        \item RL forgets less on one task and still forgets across many. What is the right objective for \textbf{continual} policy optimisation?
    \end{itemize}
