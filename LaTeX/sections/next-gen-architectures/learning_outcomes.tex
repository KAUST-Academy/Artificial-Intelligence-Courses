\section{Learning Outcomes}

\begin{frame}{Learning Outcomes}
    By the end of this session, you should be able to:
    \begin{itemize}\itemsep0.25em
        \item \textbf{Relate} decay gates, the delta rule and SSMs, and say why hybrids keep attention.
        \item \textbf{Compare} Mamba-3, RWKV, xLSTM and Hyena on recall and throughput.
        \item \textbf{Analyse} MoE routing, load balancing and serving cost at frontier scale.
        \item \textbf{Explain} how FlashAttention-3 and 4, windows and compressed sparse attention save cost.
        \item \textbf{Explain} how RoPE scaling, engineered sinks and staged training extend context.
        \item \textbf{Apply} scaling laws to a training budget, and state where they stop holding.
        \item \textbf{Describe} the current design choices in natively multimodal models.
    \end{itemize}
    \vspace{0.4em}
    {\scriptsize \textbf{Prerequisite knowledge:} linear attention, the delta rule and Gated DeltaNet; S4, Mamba and Mamba-2; MoE routing, load balancing and expert parallelism; FlashAttention; block-sparse attention (NSA, DSA); attention sinks; segment recurrence and memory-augmented Transformers; RoPE; effective-context benchmarks; Ring Attention; Kaplan and Chinchilla scaling laws; test-time compute; data curation; multimodal fusion patterns.\par}
\end{frame}
