\begin{frame}[allowframebreaks]{Summary}
  \setlength{\parskip}{0pt}
  \begin{itemize}\scriptsize\itemsep0.2em
    \item \textbf{Adapt a pretrained classifier and keep its accuracy under shift.} Fine-tuning CLIP ViT-L end-to-end lifts ImageNet top-1 from 76.6 to 86.2 but drops mean shift accuracy from 73.4 to 68.6; WiSE-FT at $\alpha=0.5$ reaches 86.8 and 76.9. LP-FT and greedy soups are other cheap fixes; CoOp, CoCoOp and Tip-Adapter adapt from 16 images per class.
    \item \textbf{Fine-grained recognition is limited by supervision as much as by architecture.} Look-alike classes need expert labels (CUB: about 4\% label errors). TransFG reaches 91.7\% on CUB and a frozen DINOv2 g/14 probe 91.6\%; BioCLIP (TreeOfLife-10M, taxonomic names) lifts mean zero-shot accuracy on 10 biology tasks from 21.9 (CLIP) to 39.4; vision-language assistants trail CLIP until trained on classification data.
    \item \textbf{Metric learning trains a distance and is judged by ranking.} Pair and triplet losses need mining; SupCon, proxies and ArcFace avoid it. Under one fair protocol (MAP@$R$), losses from 2006 to 2019 differ by a few points, while stronger pretrained ViTs lifted CUB Recall@1 from 68.4 (Proxy Anchor) to 88.8 (Unicom).
    \item \textbf{Retrieval is a two-stage system.} A global descriptor (GeM, or frozen DINOv2 at 54.0 mAP on ROxford Hard) ranks the gallery through an IVFADC or HNSW index; query expansion or SuperGlobal re-ranks the shortlist. Out of domain (ILIAS), SigLIP with one trained linear layer reaches 28.9 mAP@1k; DreamSim matches human judgments on 96.2\% of triplets.
    \item \textbf{Re-identification is retrieval of unseen identities across cameras,} scored by CMC rank-$k$ and mAP: the six-trick strong baseline with BNNeck reaches 94.5\% rank-1 and 85.9\% mAP on Market-1501, and MSMT17 mAP rose from 67.4 (TransReID) to 73.4 (CLIP-ReID) and 77.1 (SOLIDER). Deploy it only with a fixed scope, access control, a deletion schedule and human review.
    \item \textbf{Rejecting unknowns takes a score, a threshold and the right metric.} Softmax confidence tends to 1 far from the data; logit, feature and activation-shaping scores cut ResNet-50's ImageNet FPR95 from 66.95 (MSP) to 20.05 (SCALE). Report AUROC and FPR95 with ID as the positive class.
    \item \textbf{Foundation models change how unknowns are handled.} Closed-set accuracy predicts open-set AUROC (Pearson 0.95); near-OOD stays hard (ASH: 78.17 against 95.74 AUROC on far-OOD); CLIP rejects unknowns from text labels alone (NegLabel: 94.21 AUROC); SimGCD turns rejected images into new classes (60.3\% clustering accuracy on CUB).
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
